Pet care tips

General awareness · not veterinary advice

A quick reference for common and lesser-known hazards.

🍫

Chocolate

Toxic to dogs and cats — contains theobromine, which they can't metabolize safely.

🍇

Grapes & raisins

Can cause sudden kidney failure in dogs, even in small amounts.

🧅

Onions & garlic

Damage red blood cells — raw, cooked, or powdered all count, including leftovers.

🌸

Lilies

Extremely toxic to cats — even pollen on fur that's later licked off can cause kidney failure.

🪥

Xylitol

A sugar substitute in gum/peanut butter — causes a dangerous blood sugar crash in dogs.

💊

Human painkillers

Paracetamol and ibuprofen are toxic to pets even at small human doses.

🪢

String & thread

Especially risky for cats — can cause an intestinal blockage if swallowed.

🌿

Common houseplants

Pothos, sago palm, and dieffenbachia are toxic if chewed; sago palm can be fatal to dogs.

🦴

Cooked bones

Splinter easily and can injure the mouth, throat, or gut.

🕯️

Essential oils

Tea tree and many oils are toxic to cats via skin absorption or inhalation, not just ingestion.
